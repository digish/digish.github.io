Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Campfire Habits (“the App”) handles information when you use our habit-tracking and optional social “campfire circle” features.

Summary: Campfire Habits is local-first. Your habits and check-ins are stored on your device. Optional Google Sign-In connects your own Google Drive for backup and for circle sync in folders you control. We do not operate a Campfire Habits account server or sell your data.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account

The App does not require a Campfire Habits username or password. A random on-device identifier is created for circle features.

1.2 Data on Your Device

Habits, check-ins, settings, and circle membership metadata are stored locally (for example using on-device databases such as Hive). You choose which habits are private and which are linked to a circle.

1.3 Google Sign-In and Google Drive (Optional)

If you connect Google Drive:

  • Personal backup may be stored in your Drive app data area.
  • Campfire circles may use a shared folder on your Drive (or a folder you designate) to sync a circle file between members.
  • Google’s privacy policy applies to data processed by Google: policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not receive your Google password. OAuth tokens remain on your device for Drive API access.

1.4 Circle Invites

Invite links contain a circle identifier and a shared secret so friends can join. Treat invite links like passwords—anyone with the link may join that circle.

1.5 Support Email

If you email us at digish.pandya@gmail.com, we receive your address and message content to respond.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide habit tracking, metrics, reminders, and optional circle sync.
  • Improve the App and fix issues when you contact support.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Circle members see only what the App shares in the circle sync file (for example public consistency scores and linked habit activity you choose to share—not private notes).

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your Choices

  • Keep habits private and do not join circles.
  • Disconnect Google Drive from the App and revoke access in your Google account settings.
  • Delete local data from the App where the product provides data controls.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last Updated” date will change when we do.

7. Contact

Email: digish.pandya@gmail.com
