Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PurifyMedia (“the App”) handles your information. PurifyMedia helps you remove sensitive metadata from photos and videos and optionally blur sensitive content—all on your phone.

Summary: Your photos and videos are processed on your device. We do not upload your media to our servers. The App does not require an account. We do not sell your personal information. The current version of the App does not include analytics or advertising SDKs.

1. Information we do not collect

For normal use of the App, we do not collect, store on our servers, or receive:

  • The contents of your photos or videos
  • Your name, email address, or phone number (unless you email us for support)
  • Usage analytics or advertising profiles in the current App version

2. What the App accesses on your device

To work, the App needs permission to read photos and videos you choose, and to save cleaned copies:

  • Photos and videos: Only items you pick or share into PurifyMedia are processed
  • Storage (Android): Access to media and app folders so purified files can be saved under Pictures/Movies/PurifyMedia as described in the App

Your original files in the gallery are not overwritten. PurifyMedia writes new copies with a _purified suffix in the filename.

3. What stays on your device

The following may be stored locally on your phone (for example in app storage or secure storage):

  • App settings and preferences
  • Temporary working files while a job runs (removed after processing)
  • Free-tier usage counters (such as daily limits)
  • Pro purchase status so Pro features work offline after you buy or restore
  • Saved custom profiles (Pro)

This data is not uploaded to us as part of normal App use.

4. On-device processing

Metadata removal and optional content cleanup (such as blurring faces or license plates) run entirely on your device. No cloud service receives your media for purification.

5. Optional network use

Most features work without sending your media over the internet. The App may use the network only when:

  • You open the location map in the metadata editor—map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap contributors (your coordinates may be sent to the tile server to load the map)
  • You open this privacy policy or other links in your browser
  • You complete or restore an in-app purchase through Apple or Google

6. Purchases

PurifyMedia Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase through:

  • Apple App Store (StoreKit)
  • Google Play (Play Billing)

Payment details are handled by Apple or Google, not by us. We do not receive your full card number. The store may provide purchase status so the App can unlock Pro. You can restore a prior purchase in the App under Settings → Restore purchases while signed into the same store account.

7. Share extension (iOS)

If you share photos or videos from another app into PurifyMedia, the share extension passes references to those items into the main App. Media is not processed inside the share extension itself.

8. Your choices

  • Choose which photos and videos to process
  • Revoke photo access in iOS or Android system settings
  • Delete purified copies from your library or folders at any time
  • Remove local App data by uninstalling the App
  • Manage or cancel purchases through your Apple ID or Google account

9. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has contacted us with personal information we should not retain, email us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Security

Processing stays in the App’s sandbox on your device. Any data sent to Apple, Google, or map tile providers for the purposes above uses their standard secure connections.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. If we add analytics or other data collection in a future version, we will update this page and store listings before release.
