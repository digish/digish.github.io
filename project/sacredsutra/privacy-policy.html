Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 3, 2026

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Sacred Sutra app ("the App") handles your data.

Summary: Sacred Sutra is designed to work primarily offline. Reading progress, preferences, and entitlements stay on your device. We collect limited anonymous analytics to improve the App. We do not require an account and do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

The App does not require you to register, sign in, or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number to use core features (reading, search, teachings, quizzes, and related study tools).

1.2 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following may be stored only on your device (for example via SharedPreferences and a local SQLite database):

  • Reading progress and last-opened positions
  • App settings and preferences
  • Quiz and teaching interaction state
  • Feature entitlements / trial state for optional "Beyond" content

This local data is not uploaded to our servers as part of normal App use.

1.3 Information Collected Automatically

To understand how the App is used and to improve stability and features, we may collect anonymous usage analytics, such as:

  • Usage events: Screen views and feature usage (for example opening a book, starting a quiz, or using search) without collecting verse text or personal content
  • Device / app context: General device and app version information as provided by the analytics SDK
  • Diagnostics: Crash or performance signals if diagnostic tooling is enabled in a future update

2. How We Use Your Information

Information is used to:

  • Provide the App: Store preferences and progress on your device
  • Analytics: Understand which features are used so we can improve Sacred Sutra
  • Monetization (optional features): Support rewarded or other ads for optional "Beyond" access, if and when advertising is enabled in a release
  • App improvement: Identify technical issues and improve reliability

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Firebase Analytics (Google)

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage events. Firebase / Google may process device and usage data according to their policies. See Firebase Privacy and Google's Privacy Policy.

3.2 Advertising (when enabled)

Optional features may use a rewarded-ad or similar advertising SDK (for example Google AdMob) in a future or current release. If advertising is enabled, the ad partner may collect:

  • Advertising ID
  • Approximate / coarse location (as permitted by the platform)
  • Device information
  • Ad interaction and performance data

When AdMob or a similar service is used, also review AdMob Data Usage.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be processed by:

  • Google / Firebase: For analytics as described above
  • Advertising partners: Only if ads are enabled in the App build you install

5. Permissions

Depending on platform and version, the App may request permissions needed for sharing content, network access (analytics, fonts, maps tiles, or ads), or advertising identifiers. Core reading content is intended to work offline after install / asset availability.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You may:

  • Limit ad tracking / reset your Advertising ID in device settings
  • Delete locally stored App data by clearing App storage or uninstalling the App
  • Contact us with privacy questions using the email below

7. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Data Security

Data sent to third-party services (such as Firebase) is transmitted using industry-standard encryption in transit. Local data remains on your device under the protection of your operating system's app sandbox.
